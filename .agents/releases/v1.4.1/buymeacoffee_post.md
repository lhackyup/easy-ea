# EasyEA v1.4.1 Released: Administration Hub Demo Access Fix! ☕🛠️

We have just released **EasyEA v1.4.1**, a swift patch release focused on enhancing the demo and exploration experience for external stakeholders and prospective evaluators.

---

### 🌟 What's in EasyEA v1.4.1?

#### 🛠️ Seamless Administration Hub Access for Demo Users
In our previous release, users exploring EasyEA with the `viewer` (demo) role experienced an active routing redirect that sent them back to the Business Hub whenever they attempted to inspect the **Administration Hub**.

With **v1.4.1**:
- **Full Platform Discovery**: Demo visitors and external stakeholders assigned the `viewer` role can now freely open and inspect the **Administration Hub** (`⚙️ Administration`) alongside the Business, Architecture, and Governance Hubs.
- **Safe Read-Only Enforcement**: Visitors can examine the registered ArchiMate libraries, ArchiMate 3.x metamodel customizations, reference packages (`.eap`), role definitions, SSO configuration parameters, and audit trails with a dedicated, reassuring **Demo Read-Only Notice** banner (`👁️ Demo Viewer Mode Active`).
- All state-modifying actions (such as library creation, deletion, package editing, or credential modification) remain strictly disabled and protected.
